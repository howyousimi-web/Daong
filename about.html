    About DAONG

    Built for accountability, not just intentions.

    Built for Can You HackIT: The IBPAP Challenge by student volunteers.

    Our Mandate

    DAONG exists to make Philippine disaster relief spending traceable from donor to doorstep, so every peso — and every sack of rice — can be accounted for at each checkpoint.

    Verification Model

    Field coordinators log intake, transit, and distribution through a Flutter mobile app; the web console mirrors that ledger in real time for donors and LGUs.

    Human-Reviewed Flags

    When checkpoint data looks inconsistent — a long delay, a weight mismatch, a route change — it's routed to a coordinator for verification. The system flags for review; it never accuses.
